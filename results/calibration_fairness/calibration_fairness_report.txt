-- B* FORMULA-DEGENERACY CHECK -----------------------------------------
  ITA = atan( (L-50) / b* ) x (180/pi)  -- extreme when b* near zero.
  Flag threshold: |b*| < 5

  Group           n     mean_L   mean|b*|  mean|ITA|  %unstable
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  light           540     61.90      6.544      69.40      41.7%
  intermediate     97     56.85     13.203      29.04      11.3%
  dark            345     55.73      5.570      60.97      51.9%
  unknown          20      0.14      0.044        nan     100.0%

  Pearson r( |b*|, |ITA| ) = -0.6167  (p = 5.84e-104)
  Rosendahl-only: r = -0.6557  |  Vienna-only: r = -0.5808  (both p < 0.0001)
  Verdict: NEGATIVE correlation confirmed — small |b*| drives large |ITA| (DEGENERACY)

  435 / 1002 images (43.4%) flagged |b*| < 5.
  Dark group: 51.9% formula-unstable  vs  Light: 41.7%.

  KEY FINDING: The strong negative correlation (r = -0.62) means extreme
  ITA values arise substantially from near-zero b* denominators, not solely
  from true skin darkness. Pink or erythematous dermoscopic skin can produce
  ITA near -90 or +90 regardless of true lightness. The dark group has
  51.9% formula-unstable images vs 41.7% in the light group, meaning ITA
  group labels carry substantial noise from this formula artefact.
